Home Verify statements
Do this once a month, after your card statements come out.
Part 1 — On your Mac (do this first)
  1. Download each card's statement PDF: KTC (••2288), JCB (••6350), Lotus's (••6313).
  2. Put the PDFs in the 01_inbox/statements/ folder. Keep the card's last-4 in the filename (e.g. KTC_2288_May.pdf).
  3. Open Terminal in the project folder and run:
    source .venv/bin/activate && python 00_Code/run_pipeline.py
    Passwords for locked PDFs are read from your Mac Keychain automatically — nothing to type.
  4. Once it starts up, the verification is ready.
Part 2 — Review (Mac or phone)
  1. Open this page. The date stamp at the top shows when your Mac last ran.
  2. For each card tab, clear the badges:
    • 🔴 Missing in DB — on the statement but not recorded → Add to DB (or Skip).
    • 🟠 Not on statement — recorded but not on the statement → Mark as OK, or Delete with a reason.
    • ✅ Matched — already lines up, nothing to do.
Part 3 — Finish (Mac only)
  1. Back on the Mac, tap "Archive statement" on each card to move its PDF out of the inbox. (This button is hidden on the phone — it's a Mac-only step.)
  2. Done — the card is reconciled for the month.
💡 If you added or deleted rows on your phone, re-run run_pipeline.py on the Mac to refresh the match list.
